Inkspire


Creative Writing Club for Middle/High School Student


What do we do in Inkspire

Members of Inkspire write their own stories

Values/Eligibility of Our Club

We pursue following values. This is same for eligibility for being a member of Inkspire


Creativity

As a creative writing club, we want members to imagine their own idea creatively.

Enjoy Writing

Members should enjoy writing about their own idea and imgaination.

Multi-Genre

The Club's activity is not restricted to certain field of creative writing. For instance, if the member is passionate about science, they can write their own science fiction.
